# Pitch Deck & Demo Packaging

Hackathons are won on 30% Novelty, 40% Execution, and 30% Pitch Narrative.

## Pitch Structure (The 3-Minute Demo)
1. **The Hook (0:00 - 0:30):** The visceral customer pain and why current tools fail.
2. **The Mechanism (0:30 - 1:15):** The unique technical wedge / architecture insight.
3. **The Live Demo (1:15 - 2:30):** 
   - Show the happy path working live.
   - Highlight the magical wow-moment.
   - Pre-seeded data prevents any network hiccup.
4. **The Impact & Vision (2:30 - 3:00):** Market scale, immediate roadmap, team.
